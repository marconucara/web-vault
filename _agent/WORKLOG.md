| 2026-08-02 | v0.5.1 | main | ADR 0028 r2 — maps resolver cache poisoning | Fixed a rate-limited Google response being cached as a resolved place. `fetchPage` returned the last `429` after exhausting retries; `extractCoords` then matched `@lat,lng` inside the `/sorry/` page's `continue=` parameter, and both the retry filter and the usable guard accepted `lat != null` as success — so the bogus entry entered `dist/maps-cache.json` and was never retried (permanent until a `MAP_CACHE_KEY` rotation). Now: blocked responses are failed fetches, `isUsableEntry` requires title-or-image, unusable entries stay out of both caches and are re-fetched, and unresolved links log `UNRESOLVED` without failing the build. Pre-fix poisoned caches self-heal on the next build. Deliberately NOT a build-failure gate: a `429` is transient and hits every link at once. Client-side surfacing (`mapsIssues`) parked as an ADR 0028 open question. First tests for `scripts/`. See `plan/done/2026-08-02-maps-resolver-reject-blocked-responses.md`. |
